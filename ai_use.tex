\chapter*{Declaration of AI Use}
In creating this work, I have used AI tools for the following tasks:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Literature Search and ideation:} I used ChatGPT (GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.6) to identify relevant literature on zeroth-order optimization and accelerated gradient algorithms. All proofs in this dissertation were developed, verified, and written by me. 
    \item \textbf{Spelling and Grammar:} I used ChatGPT (GPT-5.6) to find typos and grammatical errors which I fixed by hand. All prose and mathematics in this dissertation were written by me.
    \item \textbf{Improvements to writing:} I used ChatGPT (GPT-5.6) to obtain feedback on the clarity of individual sentences. I then revised these sentences myself without substantive changes to their meaning or structure.
    \item \textbf{Coding:} Not used.
    \item \textbf{Plotting, formatting, etc.:} Not used.
\end{itemize}
